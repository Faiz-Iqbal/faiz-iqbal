## Selected projects

**[Amazon Review Sentiment Analysis](https://faiziqbal.netlify.app/Projects/NLP_AmazonReviewAnalysis.html)**  
I used the text of Amazon reviews to train a sentiment classifier and explore patterns in customer feedback.

**[E-puck T-Maze](https://faiziqbal.netlify.app/Projects/F20RO_Report_Group5.html)**  
Our team compared two ways to guide an e-puck through a maze. One controller followed rules we wrote. The other used a neural network evolved with a genetic algorithm.

**[Coffee Quality Prediction](https://faiziqbal.netlify.app/Projects/MLmodels_coffee_analysis.html)**  
I explored how aroma, flavour and acidity relate to coffee quality scores, then compared models for predicting those scores.
